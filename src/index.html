Library folders · scanned in priority order

Rechecks every 15 minutes and when you come back to the window. Anything that matches a show already on your shelf gets linked on its own; anything that doesn't is left for Import so nothing is guessed at silently.

Point at your anime folders — local drives and network shares both work (Show → Season 1 → files, Plex-style; Season 0 counts as specials). Filenames like S02E01, 1x01 or “Show - 05” are matched to episodes automatically. When the same episode exists in several folders, the higher folder in this list wins.

To pre-convert a show's episodes for instant playback, open the show and use Pre-convert for instant play on its Local files card (or the ⋯ menu). Converted copies live in a cache that self-prunes at 10 GB.

Export writes shows, watched history, artwork picks and keys to a JSON file. Import merges a backup into this library — nothing is deleted.
